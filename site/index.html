How the numbers are made

A submission plays each anchor in duplicate sets: every deal is played four times with the seats rotated, so luck cancels. A game pays by final position: King +1, Sub-King +0.2, Sub-Kooz −0.2, Kooz −1, tied seats sharing their positions. Every game also counts two other ways: King only pays 1 for King and 0 for anything else, and score delta is our seats' mean score minus the opponent's. Each way decides its own set winner, and each rating is one Bradley-Terry fit over every pair's set results under it, anchors included. Elo counts sets won, so a single exploding score cannot swing it. Against each anchor shows the average per game: reward, King share or delta. The bar shows each position's share, averaged over the bot's opponents. Over budget counts card decisions slower than 0.25 s.
